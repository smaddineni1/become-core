Breakfast

Spinach & Feta Omelette with Avocado

420 cal

15 min

P: 32gC: 18gF: 28g

Ingredients

  • • 3 eggs (whole)
  • • 1 cup fresh spinach
  • • 30g feta cheese
  • • 1/2 avocado, sliced
  • • 1 tsp olive oil
  • • Salt, pepper, herbs

Method

Whisk eggs, pour into heated olive oil pan. Add spinach and crumbled feta. Fold when edges set. Serve with sliced avocado on the side.

Lunch

Grilled Salmon Bowl with Quinoa & Greens

580 cal

20 min

P: 42gC: 48gF: 22g

Ingredients

  • • 150g salmon fillet
  • • 100g quinoa (dry)
  • • 2 cups mixed greens
  • • 1/2 cucumber, diced
  • • 1 tbsp tahini dressing
  • • Lemon, salt, pepper

Method

Cook quinoa per package instructions. Season salmon with lemon and herbs, grill 4 min each side. Assemble bowl with greens, quinoa, salmon, cucumber. Drizzle tahini.

Dinner

Herb Roasted Chicken with Sweet Potato

620 cal

35 min

P: 48gC: 52gF: 18g

Ingredients

  • • 180g chicken breast
  • • 1 medium sweet potato
  • • 1 cup broccoli florets
  • • 1 tbsp olive oil
  • • Rosemary, thyme, garlic

Method

Cube sweet potato and toss with olive oil and herbs. Roast at 200C for 20 min. Season chicken, pan-sear 6 min each side. Steam broccoli. Plate together.

Greek Yogurt with Berries & Walnuts

180 cal

2 min

P: 20gC: 14gF: 6g

Ingredients

  • • 200g Greek yogurt (full fat)
